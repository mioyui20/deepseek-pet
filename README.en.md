# Da Fei Yu · 大肥鱼

[简体中文](README.md) · [Download for Windows](https://github.com/mioyui20/deepseek-pet/releases/latest/download/DaFeiYu-Windows-x64.zip)

Da Fei Yu is a Windows desktop pet that keeps you company while you type. She reaches for the matching key, moves her little mouse with your pointer, and rests her cheek on her wrist when you step away.

![Da Fei Yu typing, following the mouse, napping and waking](docs/DaFeiYu-motion.gif)

## Get started

Requires **64-bit Windows 10 or 11** and **.NET Framework 4.8**.

1. Download and extract `DaFeiYu-Windows-x64.zip`.
2. Open `DaFeiYu.exe`, then try typing and moving your mouse.
3. Right-click the pet or its tray icon to open Settings. Choose **English** at the top to switch the interface.

To update, quit the current copy from the tray, then extract and run the new version. [All releases](https://github.com/mioyui20/deepseek-pet/releases)

## Everyday controls

- Drag her to a new spot or click her to give her a pat.
- After around 45 seconds without input, she takes a nap. Type or move the mouse to wake her. You can also preview the nap from the menu.
- Make her smaller, lower the opacity or turn on click-through to free up screen space. Turn click-through off from the tray.
- If she is hidden, open the EXE again or use **Show pet** or **Move to bottom-left** from the tray menu. The icon may be in the hidden icons area.

She can run alongside Guga. Each pet saves its settings separately.

## Privacy and feedback

The app runs offline. Temporary key states and mouse coordinates drive the animation. It does not record typed text. Your settings stay on your PC.

When reporting a problem, include your Windows version and the steps that led to it. [Issues](https://github.com/mioyui20/deepseek-pet/issues)
